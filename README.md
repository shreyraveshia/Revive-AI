# Revive AI

AI-powered revenue recovery and next-best-action engine.

## Problem

Failed and at-risk payments do not always represent lost customer intent.
Different failure conditions require different recovery actions.

Revive AI aims to:

1. Detect revenue at risk
2. Diagnose the likely cause
3. Predict recovery probability for available actions
4. Select the economically optimal intervention
5. Enforce deterministic safety policies
6. Execute controlled recovery workflows
7. Verify the outcome
8. Measure incremental revenue recovered
